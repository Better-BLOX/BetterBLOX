BetterBlox – a cleaner way to launch Roblox.

BetterBlox is a simple, browser‑based launcher designed to make starting Roblox sessions faster, cleaner, and more organized. No downloads, no shady installers – just a lightweight HTML page that helps you get into games with less friction.

This project is community‑made and not affiliated with Roblox Corporation. Always use your official Roblox account and follow Roblox’s terms of service.

Fast game launching

Quickly jump into your favorite experiences without digging through cluttered menus or extra steps. BetterBlox focuses on speed and clarity.

No installers, no EXEs

This page is built entirely with HTML and CSS. There are no executables, no hidden downloads, and no background processes.

Transparent behavior

All logic can be inspected directly in the page source. If you don’t trust it, you can read it – or fork it.

Community‑driven

BetterBlox is a fan‑made utility. Feedback, suggestions, and forks are welcome as long as they respect Roblox’s rules and user safety.

Safety & trust

BetterBlox does not ask for your Roblox password, does not modify your client, and does not attempt to bypass Roblox security. It is meant to sit on top of the normal Roblox experience, not replace it.

Always:

  • Log in only through the official Roblox website or app.
  • Never share your password or 2FA codes with any launcher.
  • Report anything suspicious directly to Roblox support.
